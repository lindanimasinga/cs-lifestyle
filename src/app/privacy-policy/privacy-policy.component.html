iZinga Privacy Policy

In accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 (POPIA)

1. Introduction

iZinga ("iZinga", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 ("POPIA").

This Privacy Policy explains:

  • what personal information we collect;
  • how we collect, use, store and protect it;
  • who we share it with;
  • your rights as a data subject; and
  • how to contact us regarding privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the iZinga ecosystem, including:

  • Customers
  • Delivery Drivers / Messengers
  • Driver Managers
  • Restaurant and Store Owners
  • Business Partners
  • Support Users
  • Administrators

This Policy applies to both delivery.izinga.co.za and onboard.izinga.co.za. The data processing activities across both platforms are substantially similar and therefore a unified policy is appropriate at this stage. Separate privacy notices may be introduced in future if a product develops materially different processing activities.

3. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on your role and use of the platform, we may collect the following categories of personal information.

3.1 Identity and Contact Information

  • Full name and surname
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Date of birth
  • Identity or passport number (where required)
  • Profile photograph or selfie image

This information is used for account creation, verification, communications, onboarding and support.

3.2 Account Information

  • User role
  • Business and service information
  • Approval status
  • Terms and conditions acceptance records
  • Online availability status
  • Profile information provided during onboarding

This information allows iZinga to manage access rights, workflows and operational functions.

3.3 Driver and Compliance Information

For drivers and delivery partners we may collect:

  • Identity documents
  • Driver's licence information (where required)
  • Proof of address
  • Proof of banking details
  • Vehicle information
  • Public Driving Permit (PrDP) information
  • Compliance verification documents
  • Criminal screening status information where applicable

This information assists with onboarding, compliance assessments, fraud prevention and operational safety.

3.4 Financial Information

We may collect:

  • Banking details
  • Payout information
  • Earnings and settlement records
  • Transaction references

iZinga does not store or process raw card payment information. Payment card transactions are processed by Yoco as described below.

3.5 Order and Delivery Information

  • Pick-up and delivery addresses
  • Delivery instructions
  • Order details
  • Delivery status information
  • Delivery fees and transaction information
  • Assigned customer, driver, restaurant and merchant identifiers
  • GPS coordinates associated with deliveries

This information is required to provide delivery services and manage orders.

3.6 Device and Technical Information

  • Device identifiers
  • Browser information
  • Push notification tokens
  • Login and authentication records
  • Session information

This information assists with platform functionality, notifications, troubleshooting and security.

3.7 Communications and Support Information

  • Customer support interactions
  • Chat messages
  • Attachments submitted during chats
  • Support tickets
  • Call records and communication metadata

Support interactions may be stored and reviewed for quality control, dispute resolution and service improvement.

4. How We Collect Information

We collect personal information through:

Direct collection

When you:

  • Register an account
  • Complete onboarding forms
  • Place orders
  • Upload documents
  • Update profile information
  • Interact with support services

Automated collection

Through:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Messaging
  • Browser storage
  • Device registration
  • Location and mapping services

Support-assisted collection

Information may also be collected or verified through customer support interactions.

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under POPIA, iZinga processes personal information where:

  • you have consented;
  • processing is necessary to perform a contract with you;
  • processing is required by law;
  • processing protects a legitimate interest of the data subject;
  • processing is in iZinga's legitimate business interests and does not override your privacy rights.

6. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information to:

  • create and manage accounts;
  • onboard drivers, restaurants and merchants;
  • process and complete deliveries;
  • communicate with users;
  • provide support services;
  • process payouts;
  • prevent fraud;
  • comply with legal obligations;
  • improve and secure our services;
  • resolve disputes;
  • maintain operational records.

7. Third-Party Service Providers

7.1 Yoco

iZinga uses Yoco as a payment service provider.

When you make a card payment:

  • cardholder information is collected directly by Yoco;
  • payment information is processed by Yoco;
  • Yoco is responsible for PCI-DSS compliant card processing;
  • iZinga receives transaction status, payment confirmations and reference information only;
  • iZinga does not store raw card numbers, CVV numbers or full payment card details.

Yoco processes personal information in accordance with its own privacy notices and applicable legal requirements.

7.2 Google Firebase

iZinga uses Google Firebase services, including:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Hosting
  • Firestore Database
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging

Firebase processes personal information on behalf of iZinga for authentication, hosting, messaging and chat functions. The processing may involve storage or transfer of information outside South Africa. Appropriate safeguards are implemented through Google's contractual commitments and security controls.

7.3 Other Service Providers

Depending on operational needs, iZinga may engage:

  • cloud hosting providers;
  • communication service providers;
  • messaging platforms;
  • legal and compliance advisors;
  • analytics providers.

All operators must be contractually required to implement appropriate security measures.

8. AI-Assisted Support and Messaging

Where support services are conducted through WhatsApp or AI-assisted systems:

  • support conversations may be transmitted through WhatsApp infrastructure;
  • message content may be processed by AI systems to assist support responses;
  • supporting integrations may operate through cloud infrastructure providers.

Users should avoid sharing passwords, PINs, OTPs or payment card information during support interactions.

9. Sharing Personal Information

We may share personal information:

  • with assigned drivers to fulfil deliveries;
  • with restaurants and merchants fulfilling orders;
  • with payment processors;
  • with cloud service providers;
  • with support providers;
  • when legally required;
  • during compliance investigations;
  • when protecting rights, safety or property.

We do not sell personal information.

10. Data Security

iZinga implements reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including:

  • HTTPS encryption;
  • access controls;
  • role-based permissions;
  • authentication controls;
  • secure document storage;
  • audit logging;
  • periodic access reviews.

No system can guarantee absolute security, but we continuously monitor and improve our safeguards.

11. Data Retention

iZinga retains personal information in accordance with legal, operational and compliance requirements.

Data Category Retention Period
Customer account records Duration of account plus 5 years after closure
Order and delivery records 5 years from order completion
Driver onboarding records Duration of relationship plus 5 years
Driver compliance documents Duration of relationship plus 5 years unless longer retention is legally required
Financial and payout records 5 years after financial year end
Tax and accounting records Minimum period required by applicable tax legislation
Chat and support records 3 years from closure of interaction
Identity verification records 5 years after account closure
Device and notification records 12 months after last activity
Marketing consent records 5 years after withdrawal of consent
Website authentication logs 12 months
Rejected onboarding applications 12 months unless litigation or regulatory requirements require longer retention

Where litigation, investigations, regulatory requests or legal obligations apply, information may be retained for longer periods. At the end of the retention period, information will be securely deleted, anonymised or destroyed.

12. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to:

Access

Request confirmation of whether iZinga holds personal information about you and obtain access to that information.

Correction

Request correction, updating or completion of inaccurate information.

Deletion

Request deletion of information where retention is no longer legally required.

Objection

Object to the processing of personal information where permitted by POPIA.

Withdrawal of Consent

Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Direct Marketing Objection

Object to direct marketing communications at any time.

Complaint

Submit a complaint to iZinga or directly to the Information Regulator.

Requests may be submitted to: privacy@izinga.co.za

Where cross-border transfers occur, iZinga will ensure that:

  • the recipient is subject to laws providing adequate protection; or
  • contractual safeguards are in place; or
  • another lawful POPIA transfer mechanism applies.
